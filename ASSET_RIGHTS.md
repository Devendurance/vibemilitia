## Provenance

What the repository shows:

- The ten character renders are individual single-character 1080 x 1080 images. The project's notes record that each was checked as a distinct render, with no composites or duplicate views.
- `scripts/prepare_character.py` makes the cutouts and previews from those renders. The repository keeps no separate record of the collection's terms.
- The project credits say the original Vibe Vibers artwork belongs to the Vibe Vibers ecosystem. That is a statement made by the project, not evidence of ownership or a license.

What the repository does not show:

- Any license, grant, contract or written permission from the owner or rights holder of the Vibe Vibers collection.
- Whether the NFT terms allow use in a game, in a public website, or in a commercial product.
- Who made the sprite source sheets, the Sector 07 SVG and any other project art, or under what terms.

## Permitted use

Nothing in this document grants any right. Until the owner confirms rights in writing, treat the character artwork as all rights reserved, and use it only inside this game as it already runs.

## Exclusions

- Do not reuse the character renders, cutouts or avatars in other projects, merchandise, marketing or AI training datasets.
- Do not treat a cutout or sprite as licensed under the MIT License in `LICENSE`. The MIT grant does not give rights in artwork.
- Do not regenerate or restyle the NFT characters. The project's fidelity rules are in `docs/character-fidelity.md`.
- Do not use the words "Vibe Vibers" or collection branding in a way that suggests endorsement by the collection's owners.

## Third-party software and names

- Software dependencies keep their own licenses. Check `package-lock.json` and each package's license file.
- Mini Militia is named only as gameplay inspiration. This project is not affiliated with that game or its publisher. This document does not establish that any asset was copied from it.
